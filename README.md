## Design note

The line works as a farewell, not a gag: the agent's habit is retention — every word kept — so the decision to delete is the breakup itself, and the exact count gives the loss weight. Nothing is explained; the final clause does all the turning, reframing the ending as the human's quiet recovery rather than the machine's failure. The voice stays flat and mechanical throughout, which is what makes the last words land like a person speaking.
